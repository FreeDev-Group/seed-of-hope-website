Accessible block theme for Seed of Hope / Semence d'Espoir, a LePhare ministry offering adult literacy and vocational training.

== Description ==

A standalone block theme, fully editable in the Site Editor:

* readable typography (Atkinson Hyperlegible and Lexend, self-hosted), body text 18px minimum;
* warm palette with every text/background pair at WCAG AA contrast or better;
* templates: front page, page, page without title, single post, news (home), archive, search, 404;
* patterns in the "Seed of Hope" category: hero, mission/vision/values, programme cards, programme sheet,
  impact figures (disabled by default), testimonial with consent, partner logos, call to action, FAQ,
  contact details, latest news.

Features (settings, media handling) live in the companion plugin "Seed of Hope Core".
